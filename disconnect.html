Disconnect instructions

Disconnect is an authorized local operation, not a website button.

Stop local use and have the company administrator review revocation of the Intuit connection. The local implementation sends an approved revocation request only after explicit authorization. A confirmed success replaces the refresh-token store with a token-free record; an uncertain failure retains encrypted refresh material for reviewed reconciliation.

Disconnect does not delete the app-identity store, ledger, source PDFs, logs or uploaded QuickBooks attachments. Handle those records separately under approved retention and business-record obligations. This page cannot revoke access.
