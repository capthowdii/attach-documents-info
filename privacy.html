Privacy policy

Operator: Team Z Restoration LLC. Effective date: the date this policy is first published with company approval. Privacy contact: ap@teamzrestoration.com.

Purpose and information

The local uploader processes selected timesheet PDFs, which may contain names, dates, hours and project details, and company/invoice/attachment information needed for billing-document matching, duplicate checks and verification. Operators should omit unnecessary information. The local ledger stores company/invoice identifiers, file SHA-256 fingerprints, state and attachment IDs; fingerprints are not a guarantee of anonymity.

Connection and safeguards

Accounting permission is broader than this attachment workflow. Access tokens are memory-only. Persistent refresh tokens and lifecycle metadata use Windows DPAPI protection; app credentials use a separate protected store with private directory access checks. These controls do not encrypt every PDF, ledger, log or backup. Device access, storage and backup controls must be validated before deployment. The uploader does not store an Intuit login password.

Recipients

After an approved upload, Intuit receives the PDF and relevant authorization/API information. QuickBooks users may access records under company account permissions. Richard confirms he is the only person with access to the PC and that its relevant local folder is not backed up or synchronized elsewhere. Confidential PDFs, ledger, logs and protected credential stores remain on that PC, except for approved exchanges with Intuit. This description reflects the current arrangement; changes require a privacy review. No persistent credential store has yet been provisioned. If the proposed Playground route is approved, Intuit also processes authorization and app information during bootstrap. No extra callback provider is presently configured.

Retention

Source PDFs are read in place and not copied, changed or deleted by this adapter. Verification downloads are held in memory only. The SQLite ledger has no expiry and persists until separately removed by an administrator. Uploaded QuickBooks attachments have no automatic deletion schedule here.

Structured redacted logs are disabled by default. If enabled, defaults are three files of 256 KiB each and cleanup of files older than 14 days on subsequent writes. Idle files can remain longer. Logs contain only timestamp, operation/method, result/category, HTTP status, safe error codes and validated Intuit trace ID; not bodies, URLs, headers, credentials, PDF contents or employee details. No logs are automatically shared.

Disconnect does not delete all records. Confirmed authorized revocation replaces refresh-token storage with a token-free record; failed or uncertain revocation keeps encrypted material for reconciliation. Separate app credentials are retained. Richard is responsible for access and deletion review. Company business-record obligations and legal holds govern any discretionary removal. There is no backup or synchronization of the relevant PC folder in the current arrangement. No statutory retention period or deletion deadline is assumed.

Requests and public pages

Contact the privacy address for questions, concerns or deletion review. Richard reviews requests according to applicable law and company recordkeeping obligations. Requests involving QuickBooks records are considered separately from local records. This policy does not replace applicable workforce notices or legal rights and does not promise an unverified deletion deadline.

Public-page hosting arrangement, subject to publication approval: a separate public capthowdii/attach-documents-info repository and GitHub Pages site. Only the static app-information and policy pages are included; confidential business files, uploader configuration, tokens and credentials are excluded. These pages have no scripts, cookies, analytics or third-party embedded assets. The host processes visitor request information; GitHub states it logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes. Review GitHub's privacy statement. Hosting remains subject to approval.

Intuit handling is subject to Intuit's privacy statement and applicable product agreements. Material changes require review and communication. This owner-approved policy becomes effective upon authorized publication.
