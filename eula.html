Operator and licensor: Team Z Restoration LLC. Effective date: the date these terms are first published with company approval.

Purpose and authorized use

Attach Documents is an internal Windows tool for attaching approved business timesheet PDFs to existing invoices in the Company's authorized QuickBooks Online account. Permission is for authorized company personnel, solely for this internal business purpose, nonexclusive and nontransferable, ending when the Company withdraws access. Third-party software retains its own applicable licenses.

Connection and responsibilities

Only someone authorized to connect the company account may approve Intuit access. Accounting scope is broader than attachments. The implemented workflow restricts writes to attachments and does not issue invoices, change financial values, make payments or send customer emails. Before upload, confirm company, invoice, document and authority to share with Intuit. Protect the device and credentials and report incorrect or uncertain results.

Results and limits

Duplicate checks and uncertain-result reconciliation reduce duplicate uploads but do not guarantee error-free results. Review outcomes before relying on or retrying them. IncludeOnSend is false; authorized QuickBooks users can later change sharing. Availability depends on Windows, the network and Intuit.

Data and ending use

Business records remain the Company's records. Intuit is a separate service under applicable company agreements. Stop use when authorization ends. The administrator handles revocation and local retention; disconnect alone does not erase uploaded or local records. Material purpose, access or terms changes require review.

Administration and acceptance

Richard manages access, support and deletion review. Use is limited to company-authorized internal work under these terms. No public signup, service guarantee or change to applicable Intuit agreements is offered. These approved terms become effective upon authorized publication.
